## Seven Pointed Star
Discovered in [[Places/Breland/Sharn#House Cannith Ruins|House Cannith Ruins beneath Sharn]]. Stolen from Lady Elaydren d'Vown, who hired the party to recover it. She claimed it was a family heirloom of House Cannith West. The party lied and told her the Blades got away with the schema, keeping it for themselves due to uncertainty around how House Cannith might use it.

Identified by [[People/Arguth|Arguth]] who revealed it was a very rare object containing a schema. After a couple days work, he activated the schema, revealing a hologram of a giant mechanical clawed arm, with words resembling binary flitting about.

The mechanical arm from the schema reminded [[Tumrek d'Cannith]] of the Colussi used in the war, but even more complicated. He confirmed that it is a weapon.

## Third Schema
This schema was not at the Dhakaani Jungle Ruin, its location as marked on the [[Treasure/Ancient Map|ancient map]]. There was a magical message left there by Cepha, Scourge of the Khraal.

> "Alas poor treasure hunters, you are too late. I’ve already taken anything of value from these old ruins. I hope you’re here because of general greed rather than for something specific, or you’d probably be quite frustrated! Perhaps I’d be willing to give it to you… for the right price! I am Cepha, Scourge of the Khraal. Seek me out if you dare!
> And if the name “Phaerix” means anything to you… leave or die…"

Traveling to Cepha's lair and completing "trials" granted us an audience with her. She gifted the schema to us in exchange for a favor. 

The favor was quickly called in as the village outside her temple fell under attack. A small group including the orcish hooded figure who killed Bonal Geldem in Sharn were responsible. He attacked Cepha and demnaded that she give him the schema, and threatened to kill Cepha as well as Aya Albata. During combat, he teleported away after losing his allies, without the schema.
