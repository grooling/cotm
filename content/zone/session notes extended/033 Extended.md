SESSION 33

Sul 8 Lharvion, 998 YK 

Beneath Shamukaar, the Daask butcher shop and club, a fight broke out between the party and Daask members. During the fight, Bariac was unfortunately petrified by a medusa.  After it subsided, Rik cracked a safe under Cavallah's desk. Amlyn identified a divination hag's eye necklace that Cavallah had been wearing when he died.

Inside the safe, Rik found some loot, including a faerie bottle containing a sandy substance. Idopher arrived with a dragonmarked elf, whom Zinnia recognized as having an aberrant mark associated with House Tarkanan. The elf was possibly an assassin. The room contained several other unfortunate, petrified figures—stone statues left as grim remnants of Daask stone loans. Before departing, Rik left two cigars with Idopher and the House Tarkanan assassin.

Upon examining the safe's contents, the group found 130 platinum pieces, 2,200 gold pieces, 8,000 silver pieces, 800 copper pieces, eleven 100-gold-piece gemstones, a vial of dust, leather armor, and a notebook of Daask contacts, including Harkuun Stonerunner. The group spent 125 gold pieces of their haul on a Greater Restoration spell to reverse Bariac's petrification. After splitting the remaining funds, there would be 745 gold pieces, 1,333 silver pieces, and 133 copper pieces each, but they would distribute it at a later time.

The Daask notebook revealed that Harkuun Stonerunner had been contracted by House Kavay to assassinate Haftak ir'Clarn of House Clarn. Both were members of Sharn's sixty most noble and prominent families.

The hag eye necklace was placed in the group’s bag of holding for safekeeping.
