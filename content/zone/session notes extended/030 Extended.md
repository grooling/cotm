**SAR, LHARVION 1,998 YK**  
**SHARN - KANXITH'S APARTMENT**

The group placed Kanxith's belongings inside. Zinnia and Nakaya arranged to take over the lease from Kanxith.

They discussed the schemas and decided to eventually give them to Aerenal, Petra's isolationist homeland, but chose to store them temporarily while they investigated Rik's partner, Harken Stonerunner. They secured three schemas in safety deposit boxes and kept two with Nakaya and Bariac. The safety deposit box was located in Middle Northedge in the Lower City, courtesy of House Kundarak.

**LONG REST**

**MOL, LHARVION 2-5 DAYS DOWN TIME**

Rik investigated his missing partner. He discovered that many murders corresponded with his partner's last known whereabouts, and there were connections to the Daask criminal organization.

Petra and Amlynn researched Ysgithyrwyn, a daelkyr lord associated with the Xoriat plane, or realm of madness. Unlike other daelkyr, he was more closely tied to symbionts. Amlynn received a tip and was invited to meet a contact that evening at the Drunken Dragon, a tavern in the Dura section of the Lower City.

Zinnia caroused with the upper class and inquired about Rik's minotaur friend. She learned of a possible minotaur assassin for hire in Shamukaar, located in Khyber's Gate in the Cogs district of Sharn. During her efforts, she made two contacts—Ruby and Amber, daughters of a prominent banker.

Nakaya spent her downtime writing letters and sending care packages to her family.

**SAR 7**

Zinnia purchased fine clothes for the group to wear to the tavern. That evening, they went to the Drunken Dragon. Amlynn met her contact, a half-elf, while the others observed from a distance. The contact invited Amlynn to learn more but insisted the rest of the party should not join her.

Amlynn agreed but invited Rik to follow discreetly using an eerie token—one of her teeth. Rik disguised himself as a halfling street urchin, while Petra, Nakaya, and Zinnia followed loosely behind him. The group followed the contact and Amlynn through Dura until they arrived at a nameless shop. The shop was empty except for a lantern-lit back room.

In the back room, Chester, the always smiling jester with connections to Aya Albata, revealed himself and met with Amlynn.
