### Session 29

**December 8, 2024**

The party spent six days traveling to Sterngate. During the journey, Zinnia began writing a play based on their adventures, while Rik frantically scribbled notes about his partner. Nakaya, embracing her elderly nature, spent most of the time napping. Meanwhile, Petra busied herself with building a Tesla coil rifle.

Upon arrival, Rik acquired a set of studded leather armor, and the group purchased lightning rail tickets to Sharn for 30 gold pieces.

---

**Aboard the Lightning Rail (Zor, Nymm 26, 998 YK)**

On the lightning rail, Rik challenged a goblin to an arm-wrestling match but was swiftly defeated. Nakaya, however, easily bested the goblin, much to everyone's amusement. Later, Rik slyly stole a red egg from one of his traveling companions.

Nearby, the party noticed guards from the Zolanberg Mining Company watching over some cargo, likely from nearby gem mines. The group took a break at the _Red Dragon Bar_, where they encountered a bartender named Varrakas and his daughter.

---

**Starilaskur (Far, Nymm 27)**

The party made a stop in Starilaskur, where they visited a House Jorasco enclave. There, they paid 150 gold pieces for a _Greater Restoration_ spell, which successfully reversed Nakaya's unnatural aging. Feeling rejuvenated, Nakaya accompanied Zinnia on a shopping trip where she picked out a lovely pink skirt set.

The group sold ten pounds of dragon shards for a total of 280 gold pieces—46 gold pieces each, with 50 gold set aside for Nakaya.

---

**Back on the Lightning Rail**

Back aboard the train, Nakaya and Zinnia tried a specialty drink called the _Flaming Dragon_, which caused them to burp fire. It was during this leg of the journey that they met Cormo Bigspark and Idopher Flintrabbit, two halfling con-men cowboys. Both were well-armed and claimed to be heading to Sharn for a job.

Later that evening, Cormo approached Zinnia at the bar, he could tell she was good at pickpocketing. He offered her a job: plant a mysterious object on the guards protecting the Zolanberg Mining Company's cargo. The pay was 30 gold pieces, and Zinnia accepted. She brought the plan to the party and enlisted Rik to assist her.

Zinnia's turned Rik invisible, and together they infiltrated the guards' cart. Zinnia slipped the fluid-filled vial into a guard's pocket while pretending to be tipsy. Meanwhile, Rik climbed through a hatch and spotted Idopher making his own move in a carriage with a large sealed vault. Idopher was clearly waiting for something as he opened the cargo door. The rest of the party followed Cormo after he walked through their cart towards the guarded area.

When Cormo headed back, Zinnia used her charm magic to control the situation. He paid her the promised 30 gold pieces on top of the 15 gold advance. Cormo tried to recruit Zinnia to join him and leave the party, but she turned him down.

Realizing the danger of the situation, Rik snuck into the car, flashed his badge, and warned the guards of an impending robbery. Chaos erupted as Cormo shot the vial, causing foam to explode everywhere. Petra managed to slow him for a moment, while Amlynn ensnared him in webs. Zinnia conjured an illusion of a green slaad to frighten Idopher, who panicked and attempted to detach the caboose using acid. Petra neutralized the acid, keeping the cars connected.

As the situation escalated, pterodactyls appeared, allowing Idopher to escape. Cormo, however, was subdued and left unconscious. The party tied him up and helped clean up the foam residue. Zinnia picked up Idopher's high-quality thieves' tools, which he had carelessly left behind.

---

**Return to Sharn (Sar, Nymm 28)**

Back in Sharn, the party turned Cormo over to Sergeant Dolom and received a 300 gold piece reward, earning 50 gold pieces each. Cormo was the leader of the Bladetooth Bandits, a notorious halfling gang. Before parting ways, Zinnia subtly returned the thieves' tools to Cormo but confiscated his other belongings: a pair of pistols, studded leather armor, a shortsword, and a feather token.
