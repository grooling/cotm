**Wir, Dravago 25, 998 YK**

The group fought off waves of Blade Members, Rik briefly falling in battle, but made a daring escape. They boarded Rastia's turtle elemental, Graund, and fled under a hail of arrows, setting off on a multi-day journey toward their next destination. Rastia gifted them two spell scrolls, _Sending_ and _Summon Lesser Demons_, to apologize for abandoning them during the fight.

# Handouts and Highlights
- Rik cast *Silent Image* to depict himself pissing a puddle and cowering in the theater below, distracting and confusing some of the Blades. It seems they didn't know who we were or why we were there...
- Amlyn's fleshy appendages rose from the ground restraining many Blades members and slowing their approach to the balcony
- Rik being the last one on the balcony was surrounded by Blades members and briefly fell unconscious. Bariac healed him, getting him back up so that he could make a quick getaway up the ladder
