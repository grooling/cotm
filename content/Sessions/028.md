**Sar, Dravago 28, 998 YK - Zor, Nymm 20, 998 YK**

Still in the underground Cannith facility, Rik scried on his former partner, Harkun Stonerunner, whose hand appeared to be shriveled and one of his minotaur horns was missing. He killed a noble on a lightning rail train bound for Sharn while invoking the Daelkyr "Ysgitherwyn, the lie made flesh." The party disabled an enormous warforged-like head emitting petrifying blue light, taking one of its spotlights, before finally departing the Mournland through the disorienting Grey Mist. After retrieving their cart and horses in Lyrenton, they traveled toward Sterngate, encountering an ogre-centipede monster along the way.
