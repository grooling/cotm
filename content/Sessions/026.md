**Wir, Dravago 25, 998 YK - Sar, Dravago 28, 998 YK**

The group arrived at the Field of Ruins, finding a deserted camp of the Blades at the edge of the Dead Gray Mist.  They entered the Mist and stumbled upon a small set of ruins with a lever - using Rik's Mage Hand, they pulled the lever and went down an elevator to an underground laboratory for the "Cannith Power and Water LLC".  Throughout the series of rooms were dead warforged of the Blades.  They found a few engine rooms and a large room that had old warfare machines with a blue liquid coming of out them, and a pool of lava with two construct arms putting old scraps into the lava.  While looking at the pool, burning hands emerged from the pool, attacking the group.  While the group was successful, Supercooled was downed during the fight.  Luckily, Nakaya was able to bring him back.  At the other end of the large room was a small entrance into a cave, which had several different creatures in it - modrons and pentadrones.  After attempting to trick the creatures (who recognized Amlyn's spell casting immediately) to come down the small cave entrance, and a lot of back-and-forth, the group decided they would make their way down the tunnel after getting assurance from the pentadrone that they would not be harmed as they had a cardiovascular system.
