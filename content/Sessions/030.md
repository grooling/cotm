**Sul, Lharvion 1, 998 YK - Sar, Lharvion 7, 998 YK**

The group arranged to store the schemas in [[House Kundarak]] safety deposit boxes while investigating Rik’s missing minotaur partner, Harkun Stonerunner. Rik uncovered ties to many murders and the [[Daask]] organization while investigating Stonerunner's last known whereabouts, and Zinnia got a lead on his possible location. While researching Ysgithyrwyn the daelkyr lord at the library, Amlynn was invited to the Drunken Dragon tavern to learn more. After the meeting, [[Chester]], a creepy jester tied to Aya Albata, revealed himself to Amlynn in a mysterious back room.
