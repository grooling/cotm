**Sar, Dravago 7, 998 YK**
The party exchanged magical gifts during an unexpected Ice Plane manifestation in Sharn, celebrating Eberron-mas. Chester explained that Ysgithyrwyn is his daelkyr master and advised the group to seek Cavallah at Shamukaar for leads on Harkun Stonerunner. Amlyn explained her past connections to the three hags pursuing her, and that she didn't wish to rejoin them. At Shamukaar, a butcher shop and bar, the party negotiated with Cavallah, a goblin Daask mob boss, agreeing to disrupt the Boromar Clan in exchange for information about Rik's former detective partner.
