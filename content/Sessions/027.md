**Sar, Dravago 28, 998 YK**

The modrons, being from the Perfect Order plane known as Daanvi, asked the party to help remove an opposing chaotic force within the underground facility, an agent from the Churning Chaos plane known as Kythri. The agent was not familiar with the Grey Mist, and eventually revealed himself as a green slaad, and accused the modrons of keeping him stuck there, asking the party to remove them. The party fought and succeeded against the agent of Kythri, before exploring the facility further. They revealed and accessed a scrying machine which they used to observe Phaerix and then Zelandris. At last, in the operating theater of a surgeon construct, they retrieved the fifth and final schema from the mutilated remains of a Blades Member.
