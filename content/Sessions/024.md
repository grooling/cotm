**Mol, Dravago 23, 998 YK - Wir, Dravago 25, 998 YK**

After gathering mushroom samples with the help of the Morel mushroom-folk of Lantz, the party learned a bit more about Rastia ir'Lain. Rastia recognized the group from a recent newspaper article that hailed them as heroes. They agreed to a fair contract to assist Rastia in the dangerous town of Making in exchange for splitting loot and a turtle ride to their next destination. After two days travel they arrived in Making and entered the Theater of Light and Shadow which was filled with corpses. As they searched for valuables, they triggered an illusion, summoning spectral sharks, which they defeated. Their looting was interrupted when a dozen Warforged members of [The Blades] entered the amphitheater.
