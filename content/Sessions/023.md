**Wir, Dravago 18, 998 YK - Mol Dravago 23, 998 YK**

The group touched a crystal orb and entered a liminal space where they communicated with the giant construct. Nakaya introduced them to Alfred, a Skold— protector of the Jotun, or Giants, that was instructed to flee during a losing battle with dragons. The group reconsidered their decision to fully activate Alfred. An odd woman named Amlyn Briartooth appeared, claiming neutrality. They removed the schemas and left Alfred inert in the cave. The group traveled west, encountering a ruined town inhabited by mushroom-like creatures, the Morel, who requested help dispelling a 200-ft. storm cloud. On a giant stone turtle above the cloud, they met Rastia ir'Lain, a half-elf researcher working for Aundair, whom Amlyn charmed to learn about her mission to uncover magical artifacts.
