# Bio
Name: Amlyn Briartooth 
Race, Class, and Level: Hexblood Lunar Sorcerer/Knowledge Cleric 
Alignment: NE 
Background: Hermit 

Personality Traits: I’m oblivious to etiquette and social expectations. 
Ideals: Power. Solitude and contemplation are paths toward mystical or magical power. (Evil) 
Bonds: I’m still seeking the enlightenment I pursued in my seclusion, and it still eludes me. I entered seclusion to hide from the ones who might still be hunting me. I must someday confront them. 
Flaws: I’d risk too much to uncover a lost bit of knowledge. I like keeping secrets and won’t share them with anyone. 

Appearance: A hunched frail woman of average height that appears uncomfortably human, she wears a set of long ash gray tattered robes with the hood up obscuring her face. Trinkets depicting Eberron's many moons and bones hang upon her amidst patches of moldy holes in her cloaks. Atop her head sits a crown of briars, strange purple flora, and horns, the strange crown appears to originate from under her frayed hood. A purple eyed raven sits upon her shoulder. She leans upon a gnarled wooden staff adorn with bobbles, topped with a bizarre humanoid skull. She peers at you with striking blue eyes that lay beneath her dull white-gray hair. Between the black shamanistic markings that decorate her face lays a crooked smile. 
# Backstory
Seeking a child, your parents made a bargain with a hag. You are the result of that arrangement. A coven of hags lost one of its members. Your parents were tricked and you were created to replace the lost hag.
