The group switched Lightning Rail trains in Starilaskur, where they parted ways with [[Galin Springbranch]] who gifted them brass dragon scales, before heading to Sterngate.

The party visited the House Jorasco enclave and paid 150GP for a Greater Restoration spell for Nakaya to reverse her aging after the encounter with the ghost in the Mournland.
