After a run in and fight with the hooded orcish figure outside of Cepha's Temple, the group headed to Brokentooth to find a tavern and rest. The group received some odd looks from those in town.
# Locations
## The Majestic Egg Tavern
You are not allowed to wear shoes inside this tavern.

The group stayed the night here, where they shared their personal histories and connections to the Mournland.
