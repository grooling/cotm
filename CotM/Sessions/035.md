**Mol, Lharvion 9, 998 YK**
The party pursued Harkun, leading to a chaotic market fight where he was ultimately subdued with a blueberry pie. The Watch arrived, and Rik questioned Harkun, learning about his run-in with Nine-finger Jack who ultimately traded him to Chester who put symbiotes into Harkun. Harkun warned Rik not to trust Amlyn before being taken into custody. The group then returned to Nakaya and Zinnia’s apartment to rest.

The next day, they visited the elven district of Shae Lias, where they enlisted Taen to transport important schemas to Aerenal. Later, they met with Boromar boss Wildfeet to seek an alliance, learning about dinosaur mounts for sale. They decided to take a vacation to Nakaya’s home by water ship.
