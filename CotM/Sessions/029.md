**Far, Nymm 20, 998 YK - Sar, Nymm 28, 998 YK**

The party traveled from Sterngate to Starilaskur, where they reversed Nakaya's aging and sold dragonshards. On the lightning rail to Sharn, they disrupted a heist by halfling con-men Cormo and Idopher targeting the Zolanberg Mining Company. Cormo was captured, and Idopher escaped. In Sharn, they turned Cormo, leader of the Bladetooth Bandits, over to authorities for a 300 gold reward.
