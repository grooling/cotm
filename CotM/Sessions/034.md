**Mol, Lharvion 9, 998 YK**

Seeking refuge at Velvet's Brothel, the party rested and distributed loot from the Daask fight. Using Cavallah's odd necklace, Amlyn and Petra entered a dream space and discovered a powerful Droaam hag has been manipulating Daask’s network. Sora Katra threatened to help the hags Amlyn was running from if the party were to kill any more of her underlings. The next day, they investigated Haftak ir’Clarn’s residence, only to unsuccessfully intervene as Harkun crashed a ship into the building and assassinated Haftak.
