**Sul, Lharvion 8, 998 YK**

Reviewing Rik’s files revealed that the Daask’s violent operations had roots in Droaam while the Boromar Clan had no clear leader and less aggressive tactics. The group staked out the Boromar warehouse that Cavallah asked them to hit before visiting the Boromar owned Puny Leaf nightclub. They secured an alliance with Jander Wildfeet to raid Cavallah’s operations together. Back at Shamukaar, the raid triggered a battle and Cavallah revealed his true monstrous form while familiar reinforcements arrived.
