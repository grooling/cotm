In a cavern marked with the sigil of Clan Ashmaker, Dwarven ruins had been taken over by a troupe of goblins led by Werd, their king. In an ancient, well preserved tomb hidden deeper in the ruins, the family Ashmaker were laid to rest in sarcophagi until they were raided by the party. Art on the walls of the tomb depicted the founding of the Dwarven hold.

Galgrim Ashmaker was found in the most prominent sarcophagus wearing armor and special gloves, which were taken by Petra.

Nearby were sarcophagi containing Dulnur, Bharmor, Arnura, and Belgwyn Ashmaker. They were buried with plenty of gold, and a fancy handaxe that Nakaya took. She rolled a wisdom saving throw after doing so...

Within the tomb were Dwarven jugs sealed with wax. They contained snakes, which upon opening burst out and attacked, fatally wounding Kanxith Grexhan.
