**Zor Dravago 19, 998 YK**

Half a day later, they reached the edge of the Glass Plateau and descended. They saw a town with a 200-ft storm cloud hovering approximately 150 ft above it. Humanoid figures were moving about in the ruins of the town. The group stealthily approached. This town was more ruined than other towns in the Mournland, and southern-spotted toadstools grew around. Amlyn ate one safely and harvested a pound’s worth.

A mushroom-like figure walked by, appearing scared and defensive. Nakaya greeted it kindly, and Amlyn and Rik leaned in as well. The figure released spores on them, allowing telepathic communication. It asked if they were with the storm cloud. They replied no.  
"Can you make the cloud go away?" it asked.
Nakaya asked why.  
"It is hurting the spores."

Amlyn yelled at the cloud to "GO AWAY." It rumbled in response but did not move. The cloud seemed centered over the town. Cairo the hawk spotted a building with a red roof nestled in the cloud

The mushroom-folk, known as the Morel, explained that the spores were their young. The Morel figure was unaware of the house within the cloud.

Amlyn collected some of their spores in a jar.

The group traveled through the town toward a rope ladder dangling from the cloud. They climbed up and discovered they were on the back of a giant stone turtle that was alive. It wasn’t raining there. Amlyn and Petra approached a crystal ball altar. Amlyn gazed into the crystal ball and saw the land below. Petra peered into the window of the red-roofed house, where a half-elf woman sat writing. She was startled when Amlyn knocked on the door and asked them to leave, saying she was conducting research for her superiors. She reacted poorly to their mention of being from Sharn. Amlyn subtly charmed her, revealing her name as Rastia ir'Lain. She worked for an Aundairian research division, under a major army general or political figure from House Lain, and was searching old ruins for magical discoveries. She had been there for a few days.
