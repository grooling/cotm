SESSION 34 

**SUL LHARVION 8**  
**SHARN – Velvets Brothel**

The group headed to Velvets, a brothel owned by Velvet, Zinnia's giff mistress friend. Zinnia paid 2 platinum pieces for the Entertainment Suite, which featured a massive bed, various toys, and an orb that projected the fantasies of anyone who touched it. She ordered room service while Petra identified the faerie dust vial as a dust of disappearance. Amlyn discovered and prepared a bubble bath, and the group distributed their loot. Zinnia took a bath while Rik studied the handwriting in the Daask book.

Petra and Amlyn used the hag eye necklace to enter a dream space. Within this surreal, ever-changing environment, a maiden playing a harp introduced herself as Frieda. She asked about her friend in Dal Quor, the plane of Cavallah, revealing she had given him the necklace in Aundair. Petra broke the news that Cavallah was dead.

Frieda appeared evasive, prompting the group to admit they were responsible for Cavallah's death. It became clear she had been using him for her own purposes. She also revealed her familiarity with Amlyn, mentioning she hailed from Droaam and was fleeing from hags. Frieda's true identity was unveiled: she was Sora Katra, a green hag and a public figure in Droaam. As part of a coven with her sisters—Sora Maenya, an Annis hag, and Sora Teraza, a dusk hag—she wielded significant power.

Sora Katra threatened the group, warning them not to lose the necklace, or she would aid Amlyn’s pursuers. The dream ended with her clicking sound. Amlyn speculated that the necklace was made of dragonshard rather than a traditional hag eye, as it seemed to protect Sora Katra from vulnerability.

Sora Katra’s name appeared in the Daask book, coded among directives she had given. It became evident she was building an information network and pulling the strings behind Daask, with cells operating across Khorvaire. The group eventually rested.

**MOL LHARVION 9**  
**SHARN – Upper City**

The group located the residence of Haftak ir’Clarn, where two guards stood watch outside. Zinnia cast invisibility on Rik, who scaled and entered the building to scout the house. Inside, Rik explored several rooms before finding a man writing at a desk. The man reacted to the sound of the door opening but returned to gazing out the window, unaware of Rik’s presence.

Suddenly, shouts from the north disrupted the calm: "It’s out of control!" A ship was barreling toward the building, clearly about to crash. The group alerted the guards and rushed in as the ship collided with the residence. Harkun emerged from the wreckage, declaring to Haftak, "Prepare to die!"

Rik became visible while attempting to stop Harkun, who recognized him and warned him to step aside. Rik refused, and a fight ensued. Harkun stepped into a wall, reappearing to slash at Haftak’s hand and deliver a fatal blow. As Haftak died, Harkun’s eyes rolled back to reveal a lizard-like eye that briefly focused on Amlyn.

Before leaping out of a window, Harkun announced that his job was done. Zinnia quickly gave Rik her feather token and recast invisibility, allowing Rik to pursue Harkun by diving after him out of the window.
