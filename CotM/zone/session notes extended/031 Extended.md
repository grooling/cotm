**SESSION 31**  

The events rewound a few days to a surprise manifestation of the Ice Plane in Sharn, where the group exchanged gifts. Bariac gave Zinnia a Cloak of Fabulousness. Rik gave Petra a hovel of Dowsing. Petra gave Amlyn a Snuggle Beast voodoo doll. Nakaya gave Rik an Investigator’s Glass. Amlyn gave Bariac a Beer Weird. Zinnia gave Nakaya a Shard of Risia. Merry Eberron-mas!

**SAR 7**

Chester referred to the half-elf contact as Jimothy. He mentioned that Violet, Maggie, and Ethel were after Amlyn. Chester revealed a master who kept him well-informed: Ysgithyrwyn. Petra detected transmutation magic on Jimothy and illusion/evocation magic on Chester. Petra warned Amlyn about Chester being a stalker.

They offered Amlyn a symbiont, which would burrow into her arm and allow her to shoot magical projectiles at people, provide protection from the hags, and give her information about Stonerunner Markus. The spell worm burrowed into her arm. Chester advised them to ask for Cavallah to search for Harkun Stonerunner and to inquire about Blood Gin, a potent necrotic drug from Dream. Chester said the hags were actively pursuing Amlyn. Petra detected evocation and a hint of divination magic from Markus.

**Shamukaar**

Amlyn explained why she was being followed and why she had left the hags. She didn’t want to fully embrace her hag nature. Though she wasn’t entirely sure of her history, she knew the hags had helped her family have a child. She fled after a while, but now the hags wanted her to rejoin them.

The group entered Shamukaar in the Cogs, drawing some attention—especially everyone except Rik and Amlyn. Mandy was working the bar and called out to Zinnia. When Zinnia asked about Cavallah and Blood Gin, Mandy looked concerned. Nakaya bought shots and manticore meat for Bariac. Mandy led the group to a lower-level tavern, where patrons were doing drugs rather than drinking.

Zinnia recognized Dreamlily, a narcotic with disorienting euphoric effects and Heartslow, a drug functionally akin to cocaine. Later, she noticed shamans injecting dragon’s blood, a drug that enhanced arcane abilities.

In a private room, guarded by a male medusa guard, they found a goblin mob boss—Cavallah. Rik asked about Stonerunner and noticed Cavallah recognized the name. Amlyn revealed Markus, which visibly disgusted Cavallah. Cavallah appeared to be part of Daask organization. Rik offered a quid pro quo: in exchange for information on Stonerunner, they would hinder the Boromar Clan by targeting a few of its agents. The party agreed. Cavallah gave Rik a firm handshake and directed them to a warehouse in the Stores.

Zinnia purchased 5 GP worth of Dreamlily.

**The group retired for the night.**
