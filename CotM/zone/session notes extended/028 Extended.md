**Underground Cannith Facility**

Rik scried on his old partner, Harkun Stonerunner, a minotaur detective from Drogam and Rik Raper's former colleague. They had once been tracking a serial killer who sent victims' bones, carved with runes, to their families. Many victims had been identified, but only one body had ever been recovered.

In the scrying vision, Rik recognized the interior of a lightning rail carriage. Harkun appeared seated on a bed, missing one of his horns, with one hand shriveled and missing several fingers. Moving through the train, Harkun tore into a first-class compartment. His voice sounded strange and gurgling, and a tentacle erupted from his wrist, killing a noble. In the moment before the vision ended, Rik heard Harkun say, _"Ysgitherwyn sends his regards."_ Through the window, Rik saw the train approaching the city of Sharn.

The group realized the name _Ysgitherwyn_ referred to a Daelkyr known as "the lie made flesh." Rik concluded that his partner had been corrupted and was no longer the man he had once known. Determined to confront Harkun, the group decided to seek him out in Sharn.

Within the facility, they discovered a room full of stone figures, including a Blades member. Nakaya had almost been petrified as well, illuminated by an eerie blue light. At the center of the room was a massive, warforged-like head emitting the blue light from its eyes. The party disabled the construct by unplugging its power source and removed a spotlight-like device from behind a glass pane, adding it to their bag of holding.

After taking a short rest, the group departed the facility into the grey mist.

---

**Through the Grey Mist**

In the mist, Nakaya felt blinded and could not see the compass. Zinnia's eye twitched, and she began to tremble, while Bariac shouted in confusion and pulled away. The others helped guide their companions through the strange phenomenon. The effects persisted until Amlyn restored Nakaya's vision.

The group took six days to travel to Lyrenton, where they retrieved their cart and horses. Along the way, they encountered military patrols who exchanged knowing looks, though nothing was said.

They then spent another six days traveling to Rhukaan Draal, where they sold their loot for a total of 930 GP, earning 155 GP per party member. From there, they departed for Sterngate.
