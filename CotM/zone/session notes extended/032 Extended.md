**SESSION 32**  
**01-05-2025**

**Sul 8 Lharvion 998**

The group reviewed Rik's files on Daask and Boromar. Daask was found to have ties to Droaam, where their higher ranks appeared to reside. They operated violently and conducted “stone loans” using medusas to petrify loved ones as collateral. Boromar, by contrast, had no clear leader and was of a similar size to Daask but less violent, preferring to hire others to do their dirty work.

The party first staked out the Boromar warehouse in the Stores district, near the docks. The area was bustling, with a cluster of four buildings that appeared empty but were guarded by numerous armed individuals. Many doors were securely locked.

Next, they visited the Puny Leaf nightclub in Lower Dura, owned by the Boromar Clan. From the outside, it resembled a tea shop with a queue at the entrance. Zinnia approached an earth genasi bouncer, claiming she had information for his boss about Cavallah. He made the group wait in line, but they eventually entered after showing their ID papers. The bouncer used a notebook without ever needing to turn the page...

Inside, the nightclub was lively. Amlyn checked out the gambling tables, Rik searched for anyone who looked like a leader, and Petra noticed an unmarked door. A singer performed blues music on stage. Petra and Rik overheard the bartender using thieves' cant in Halfling to discuss the group. Rik confronted her in thieves’ cant, securing an audience with her boss.

They were led through the kitchen to a well-decorated office adorned with dinosaur head trophies. The boss, Jander Wildfeet, offered them drinks that prevented lying. Rik briefed him on the situation and asked for his assistance. Jander agreed to investigate their tip and later discovered they had been hired to hit the warehouse. Grateful they had come to him first, he requested Cavallah's location so the Boromar Clan could plan a raid. The party agreed to collaborate, and Jander gave them magical bracelets that allowed them to disguise themselves.

The group returned to Shamukaar, where they saw Boromar Clan members disguised as goblins. A different barkeep was on duty. The group asked for Blood Gin from Cavallah, and they were directed to the back. Mandy was downstairs and asked Zinnia if she had completed her job. Zinnia lied, saying, “Yes!”

The group soon heard the Boromar Clan launching their raid upstairs. Mandy realized they were being deceived, and a fight broke out. Cavallah eventually emerged from his office, transformed into a larger creature as Cormo and Idopher arrived downstairs.
