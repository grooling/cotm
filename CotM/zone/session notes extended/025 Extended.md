**In-Game Date:** Dravago 25, 998 YK  
**Location:** The Theater of Light and Shadow, Making (Mournland)

The Blades members split up, searching for the group.

Bariac pulled up the rope ladder while Rastia cast _Invisibility_ on herself and Petra. Rik created an illusion of himself downstairs near some of the Blades members, drawing their attention. Some reacted defensively, and a senior leader among them asked who he was. When the leader attempted to grab the illusionary Rik, his hand passed through, revealing the trick. Initiative was rolled.

The battle went on for a long time. Eventually, Bariac dropped the ladder down to their balcony, allowing the group to escape. They rushed up to the roof, though Rik was struck down by one of the Blades members as he was last on the balcony. Bariac healed him, and Rik dashed up to safety, allowing them to pull the ladder up just in time. The turtle took off with them on board as arrows soared by, narrowly missing.

The group then showed Rastia their destination, and she steered Graund toward it. The journey would take a few days. Rastia gifted the group two spell scrolls: _Sending_ and _Summon Lesser Demons._
