MOL DRAVAGO 23 998

On a giant stone turtle-cloud above the Mournland, above the ruined town of Lantz, inhabited by the Morel mushroom-folk.

Rastia had been to Broken Tower and Lantz. She wanted to go to Making because she believed it hadn’t been picked over yet. Zinnia convinced her to let them help, and they moved on to Making, a major seat of House Phiarlan. Rastia wanted spores and mushroom samples, so they descended while Zinnia stayed behind to keep an eye on her. Nakaya explained to a myconid what Rastia wanted to make the cloud leave. The myconid allowed them to take three vials of spores, and Amlyn left with another myconid. Bariac collected a bag of mushrooms. Amlyn went to commune with the sovereign, the “central node.” They returned to the cloud, and Amlyn rejoined the group.

Zinnia told Rastia about the battlefield they had freed. Rastia excitedly retrieved a newspaper containing an article about them, calling them heroes and mentioning Zinnia, Rik, and Barriac by name (for musical performances and a great meal). Rastia had been gone from home for two and a half weeks. The turtle was named Graund, and she had nearly been mugged in Broken Tower.

The group returned and handed over the specimens, checked out the newspaper, and then Rastia went toward the turtle's head, calling out in a strange language that made it lurch and begin moving. Amlyn telepathically informed them that a spell was about to run out. “What spell?” Petra asked. Petra proposed that Rastia hire them to help in Making. Rastia snapped out of the spell and accused them of charming her, which they denied. She seemed to agree, offering them a cut of the loot, a ride to the last schema, and major items for her and Aundairian interests. They signed a contract that seemed fair and good.

It took two days of travel to reach the destination. Rastia advised them to prepare, so they rested and took some downtime. Rastia explained that House Jorasco had a pricey Greater Restoration spell that could reverse Nakaya’s aging. They took a long rest.

WIR DRAVAGO 25 998 YK

They returned to the Glass Plateau to visit Making. It had a fortress at the center, appearing spiky, with spires of glass rising around it. There were no signs of life. Zinnia spotted an amphitheater she recognized—the Theater of Light and Shadow—now grown over with glass. They parked over it, and Amlyn and Bariac stayed back as Rastia joined them in climbing down. The theater seats and orchestra were filled with corpses, and a few lay deceased on the stage as well. Zinnia and Rik searched the pockets of the wealthy patrons in the front rows, collecting 60 gp, 800 sp, and four beautiful necklaces worth 50 gp each (totaling 200 gp). Each person received 8 gp and 114 sp, with Zinnia keeping the necklaces. Rastia detected illusion magic over the stage, creating aquatic projections that might have been prolonged by the Mourning. The costumes were aquatic in nature. Suddenly, three spectral sharks floated down from the projection and attacked! They fought a ghost hunter shark and two ghost reef sharks.

Afterward, they checked the box seats and found 3,200 cp (457 cp per person), along with gems: two quartz, two jasper, and two star rose quartz, each worth 50 gp (totaling 300 gp), as well as Keoghtom's ointment. Hearing voices below, they ducked to hide, observing about a dozen Warforged with rough gear and tabards bearing the insignia of The Blades.
